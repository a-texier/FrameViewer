# Demo data attribution

The RGB and thermal images in `traffic_rgb/` and `traffic_ir/` are excerpts
from the **AAU RainSnow Traffic Surveillance Dataset**.

- Creators: Chris Holmberg Bahnsen and Thomas B. Moeslund
- Publisher: Aalborg University / Kaggle
- Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.34740/kaggle/dsv/105294
- License recorded by the distributed dataset metadata: CC BY-SA 4.0
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The files have been renamed only to keep paired RGB, thermal and YOLO examples
aligned. The annotation examples are derived from the dataset annotations.
